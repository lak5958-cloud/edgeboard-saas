# EdgeBoard slate checklist · November 4, 2026 (Eastern)

## Before the games

- [ ] Open Daily Picks and select NBA and DraftKings or FanDuel.
- [ ] Expand **Today and tomorrow · slate readiness** to see quote, roster and history coverage by game.
- [ ] Verify November 4 matchups and recent quote timestamps. Check the exact line and price at your chosen sportsbook.
- [ ] Open Research for any selection you are evaluating. Check roster identity, availability and historical workload. Treat unconfirmed availability as unknown.
- [ ] Read the reason for Lean or Pass at that price. A Lean cleared the research checks and the accuracy evidence is still limited.
- [ ] The scheduled refresh saves NBA trade trial forecasts in the 60–45 minute window before tipoff. No manual action or open browser is required.

- [ ] In Research, open Frozen pregame audit and choose NBA trade shadow.
- [ ] Enter November 4 and choose Review frozen predictions.
- [ ] Expand Pregame capture status. Check the game and sportsbook. If there was no in-window scan or storage finished late, the capture window was missed. If a scan ran, use the counts to distinguish missing quotes, roster evidence, or history.
- [ ] Check captured lines for verified final outcomes. If pending, check after the next stats refresh.
