# Outside Picks and frozen research

Outside Picks now collects public Covers NBA/NFL article links from the publisher index during the owner refresh pipeline, at most hourly when the refresh time budget permits. The verified adapter extracts explicit Over/Under player-prop labels, matchup, article slate date, original publication timestamp and analyst attribution. It stores factual selections and links rather than reproducing the article's analysis. Inclusive “20+” labels, unsupported markets, unrecognized layouts and missing dates are skipped. The article URL slate date is separate from its publication/updated date. Original prices are marked unverified rather than copied from advertisements or current bookmaker offers.

The initial retained feed contains 11 verified November 2 NBA selections. They are archived, not current picks. Publisher outages and unsupported pages are reported; prior dated entries retain their timestamps. No login/paywall bypass, private social-group collection, or invented analyst track record is used. The current automatic adapter is Covers; other publishers require verified adapters before importing selections.

Comparison uses only a currently offered DraftKings or FanDuel price for the exact dated matchup, player, statistic, side, period and line. Lean means “Agrees at this price.” Negative modeled expected return means “Disagrees at this price,” without recommending the opposite wager. Unmet gates remain Pass, and unavailable data remains unavailable. Opinions never increase model confidence or alter its numerical inputs. Unsegmented article prop sections are treated as full-game research; the card explicitly asks the reader to confirm sportsbook settlement rules.

A seven-day archive control makes historical source picks discoverable without presenting them as today's recommendations. Board navigation carries the source slate date to prevent silently matching yesterday's pick to another game.

Each successful refresh now indexes newly stored Daily Picks and game-market reports after their immutable objects have been written. The index retains up to 20,000 objects and records snapshot time, slate date, kind and model version. The read-only audit date picker evaluates player shortlist snapshots, sampling the earliest indexed snapshot per UTC hour, with the original recovered November 2 sources retained. Game reports are archived but are not yet graded by that player-only audit. This is shortlist monitoring, not complete-market selection-bias correction, independent calibration or profitability validation. Model gates and coefficients are unchanged; multi-day evidence is needed before comparing a calibrated challenger.
